## One real-device journey

1. Open the game on the phone over its usual Wi-Fi or mobile connection. The
   Blender Kitty should load without a blank screen. Try Battery friendly detail
   in Settings if movement feels slow; record the original setting and behavior.
2. Choose Play a mission → Moonlight tea party. Walk north from the glade entrance
   using the direction pad. Mint should enter the basket automatically near the
   sprigs. Hold, slide, release and cancel touch. Kitty must stop when released;
   turning should respond promptly, and the camera should remain steady.
3. Choose Bake a cookie. Pick a cutter, icing and sprinkles. Reload midway and
   reopen baking: the cookie should remain. Give it to Momo. No timer or penalty
   should appear. Return to the glade and approach the pink table to the east.
4. Place one cup, reload, then reopen Tea-party progress and finish setting the
   table. The ending should show Kitty and Momo together with the chosen cookie.
   Reopening the ending must not add another reward.
5. Return to Her room. Tap the new teacup to read its storybook page. Open Photo
   corner, pick a pose/background and save a PNG using Safari’s download controls.
   Open Play together → Your keepsake wall: check the image, outfit, place and
   local date/time. Reload and verify the frame remains in the room.
6. Choose Play together → Gently pet Kitty. Stroke her, release, then choose Done
   petting. Petting must not rotate the room. Drag after finishing to check that
   rotation works again. Try High five and A teddy cuddle with sound both off/on.
7. In Settings select Extra large reading. Read Nancy’s letter, scroll to its end
   and close it. Rotate the phone through portrait/landscape: no horizontal
   scrolling, unreachable close buttons, clipped actions or lost progress.
8. Choose Hide interface, enjoy/rotate the scene, then tap Show interface. On Mac,
   repeat with H and Escape. Reopening the game should show the normal interface.
9. Repeat movement, cookie play, letter scrolling and PNG saving in Mac Safari.
   Leave/reopen the tab and verify progress. If transferring to another device,
   use the backup/restore flow and check the tea memory, postcards and reading size.

For a failure, record the device/browser, game build, exact action and expected
versus actual result. Note whether sound was enabled and whether the tab had just
returned from the background. Screenshots or screen recordings are useful when
available, but they are not required to record a problem.
